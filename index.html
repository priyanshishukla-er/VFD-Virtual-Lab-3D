VIRTUAL LAB EXPERIMENTS & WIRING SCHEMATICS
▲
EXP 01
V/f Speed Control & Slip Analysis

Observe how rotor RPM varies linearly with inverter frequency. Increase mechanical load to watch rotor slip increase as the rotor pulls back from synchronous speed.

EXP 02
Ramp Time & Inrush Current Dynamics

Set short acceleration time (0.5s) vs long acceleration time (5.0s). Watch peak starting current spikes on the current meter during acceleration.

EXP 03
Overload Thermal Trip & Drive Reset

Inject an overload condition or raise load to 125%. Observe the VFD trip with fault code E.OL. Use the RESET button to clear the fault.

EXP 04
Direction Reversal & Phase Sequencing

Toggle direction between Forward and Reverse. Notice how the VFD safely decelerates the motor to 0 Hz before smoothly reversing phase sequence to reach target speed.
